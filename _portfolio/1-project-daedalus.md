At UC San Diego's Rocket Propulsion Laboratory (RPL), I'm on **Project Daedalus** with **Team Centauri**, a 4–6 person team designing and building a G-class model rocket, flying an **AeroTech G80T-7** motor, for a **Spring 2027 launch**. My main focus is avionics, and I'm also part of the structures group. After Daedalus, I plan to move on to **Project Phoenix**.

Avionics
======
I'm building the avionics that tell the rocket when to deploy its recovery system. An **Arduino Nano** reads a **barometer** to calculate the rocket's altitude during flight and triggers the recovery system at the right point.

I also plan to use the same sensor data to **log kinematic data** during the flight, then retrieve and analyze it after recovery.

Structures
======
- **Fin can & motor mount:** Designed the fin can and motor mount for the rocket.
- **Nose cone generator:** Wrote a MATLAB script that generates spline profiles for custom **Von Kármán (or any Haack series) nose cones**. It exports the profiles as CSV files that import straight into Fusion for modeling.
