At the Idea Center Makerspace in Ventura, I worked as a 3D printing intern from August 2025 to May 2026.

Printer Operation & Maintenance
======
I ran, calibrated and maintained the makerspace's **FDM 3D printers** so they produced dimensionally accurate prints. When prints failed, I tracked down and fixed both **printer hardware and slicing problems**, making prints better and more reliable.

Designing for 3D Printing
======
I tuned CAD models and slicer settings for 3D printing using **Prusa Slicer, SolidWorks and OnShape**. I also worked on the **gearing assembly for the 3D Printed Wave Machine display**.

Mentoring
======
I mentored K-12 students through the engineering design and prototyping process and enforced lab safety rules.
